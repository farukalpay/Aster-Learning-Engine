# Commercial Use

Aster uses an open-core posture.

The engine core is available under Apache-2.0. You may use the core source,
public ABI, Game SDK, asset compiler/runtime code, tests, and build scripts to
build and ship commercial games or applications. Aster does not charge a runtime
royalty for those shipped games or applications.

Aster-owned content is separate. Sample projects, showcase assets, screenshots,
Lumen Run content, branded material, and Aster marks are not automatically
licensed for commercial reuse. Use your own content for commercial releases, or
ask for written permission for commercial reuse of Aster-owned content.

Paid Aster paths may include support, consulting, renderer/backend integration,
certification, private platform work, Studio Pro, proprietary tooling,
commercial asset packs, and commercial use of Aster-branded content.

This file is a product and licensing summary, not legal advice. The license files
remain the controlling terms.
